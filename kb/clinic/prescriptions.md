# Prescriptions and Refills

## Requesting a refill
Request refills through the patient portal. Allow 3 working days for a refill to be processed.

## Before travelling
If you are travelling, request your refill at least 7 days before you leave.

## 90-day supplies
Patients on stable long-term medicines can ask for a 90-day supply.

## Changes to your medicines
The clinic does not change medicine doses through portal messages. Dose changes need an appointment or a call with a nurse. Do not stop or change a medicine without speaking to your care team.
