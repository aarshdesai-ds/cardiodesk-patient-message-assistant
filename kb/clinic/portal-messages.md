# Using the Patient Portal

## Response times
Portal messages are answered within 2 working days. The portal is not monitored in the evenings, at weekends or on public holidays.

## What the portal is for
Use the portal for routine questions, refill requests, appointment requests and test results.

## What the portal is not for
Do not use the portal for urgent or emergency symptoms. Messages may not be read for up to 2 working days.

## Nurse line
For same-day concerns, call the nurse line on weekdays between 8 AM and 5 PM.
