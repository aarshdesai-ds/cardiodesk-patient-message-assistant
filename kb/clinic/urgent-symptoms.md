# When to Get Help

## Call emergency services now
Call your local emergency number immediately for chest pain or pressure, severe trouble breathing, fainting, or sudden signs of a stroke such as face drooping, arm weakness or difficulty speaking. Do not wait for a reply on the portal.

## Call the nurse line the same day
Call the nurse line the same day for new or worsening swelling, palpitations that do not settle, new dizziness, or symptoms that are getting worse.

## Routine questions
For questions that are not urgent, send a portal message or book an appointment.
