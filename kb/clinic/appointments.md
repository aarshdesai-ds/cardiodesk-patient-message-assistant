# Appointments

## Booking
Book appointments through the patient portal or by calling the front desk on weekdays between 8 AM and 5 PM. New patients are usually seen within 2 weeks. Follow-up visits are usually available within 5 working days.

## Telehealth
Video visits are available for follow-up appointments and medicine reviews. A first visit must be in person.

## Cancelling or rescheduling
Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before the appointment, through the portal or by phone.

## What to bring
Bring a list of all your current medicines and supplements, and any home blood pressure or weight readings.
